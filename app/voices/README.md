# Live voices

Without a voice pack the casters speak live with Kyutai Pocket TTS, copying the delivery of
six short reference clips put here:

    male-hype.wav  male-lively.wav  male-calm.wav
    female-hype.wav  female-lively.wav  female-calm.wav

Each one is 8-12 seconds of one person talking (WAV, mono). They aren't in this repo: use
clips of your own voice, or of someone who has agreed to it. The release exe ships with its
own set. With a voice pack (see studio/), these aren't used at all.
